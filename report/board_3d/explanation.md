## Explanation

The question asked for the blackboard's text, and the blurred panorama showed the dark board directly ahead, at roughly yaw -20 to +15 and pitch 0 to -15. So the plan was to tile the board with sharp foveal views, read line by line, and transcribe as I went.

Fixation #2 at (-10, 0) found a vertical divider line splitting the board into two sections. To its left was a column of words: "...ka", "...n chat", "...apa".

Fixation #3 at (-18, -1) reached the board's left edge. It showed the single letters m, r, l with "Mika", "un chat" and "papa", plus a faint letter at the bottom.

Fixation #4 at (-16, -9) looked lower in the left column and added "maman". Its leading letter stayed faint, which is why it appears as "?" in my answer. Below that were only ruled lines, so the left section was finished.

Fixation #5 at (4, 1) went back to the right section and caught "Jeudi 1...". It also showed circled letters cut off at the right edge of the view.

Fixation #6 at (12, -2) completed the date as "Jeudi 13 septembre". It also read the first circled syllables: ma, li, na, si.

Fixation #7 at (12, -9) moved down through the syllables and found pa, la, a partial li/i, si, and a circled item at the bottom.

The scanpath had some inefficiency. Fixation #1 cut text on both sides, and fixation #5 partly repeated it. A slightly more rightward or lower placement could have combined #6 and #7. I could have spent a few more fixations, for example zooming in on the faint letter, but I chose to stop once the board had been covered.
