# How many chairs are there? (3D)

**Answer.** 22

**Summary.** I read the plan as a 4×5 grid of student desks, each with a seat. I then looked for chairs outside that grid. Fixations 2 and 3 found a chair by the front-left wall and one behind the teacher's desk. Fixation 4 confirmed that the student seats are separate chairs. Total: 20 + 2 = 22.

## Explanation

The room is a classroom with many near-identical desk-and-chair units. Fixating and marking every chair one by one would have cost many fixations. So I relied on the plan's regular layout for the bulk of the count and used the fovea only for chairs outside that pattern.

Fixation 1 (straight ahead) showed the blackboard. The plan showed a 4×5 grid of desk units, each with a seat outline, plus a possible teacher chair at the front left. That set up the plan: check the front of the room for any extra chairs.

Fixation 2 (-19, -10) found a standalone brown metal-frame chair against the front-left wall, about 6.4 m away. I marked it as chair 1.

Fixation 3 (12, -6) looked at the teacher's desk on the front right. A wooden chair back was visible behind it, and I marked it as chair 2.

Fixation 4 (20, -40) checked a nearby student unit to settle whether the student seats are separate chairs or built into the desks. I saw a separate wooden seat and backrest on a metal frame. I marked that chair as 3 and concluded every grid unit has its own chair. That gave 20 student chairs plus the 2 front chairs, for an answer of 22.

Weaknesses:
- Only one of the 20 student chairs was actually inspected. The "20" is inferred from the plan's grid, not counted chair by chair. Missing or extra chairs in the grid, especially in the blurred back and side areas, would go unnoticed.
- I never looked at the rear of the room (behind me, near ±180°) or along the side walls for stray chairs.
- Only 3 of the 22 chairs carry marks, so the mark count does not match the stated answer.

A more thorough scan of the desk rows and the rear of the room would have made the answer more reliable.
